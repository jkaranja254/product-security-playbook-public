# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project aims to be respectful, practical, and constructive.

Examples of behavior that help maintain that standard include:

- using welcoming and professional language
- assuming good intent and giving clear feedback
- focusing discussion on ideas, evidence, and improvements
- respecting different perspectives and experience levels

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- publishing private or confidential information
- hostile, dismissive, or intentionally disruptive conduct

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, or contributions that do not align with this standard.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately with relevant context.
